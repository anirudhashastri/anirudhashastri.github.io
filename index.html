05 / About
“I like problems where computation has to survive contact with reality.”

My work sits between machine learning and clinical workflows, where model performance is only part of the problem. Registration error, image geometry, annotation quality, uncertainty, inference time, and clinical usability all matter.

Outside the lab, I’m usually exploring tools, cameras, cycling routes, 3D printing, electronic repair or the infrastructure that makes technical work possible.
